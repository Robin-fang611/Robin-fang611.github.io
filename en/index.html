05

Method

Four operating principles that can be executed — and argued with.

  1. 01Go on site before proposing

    Off site, you only see the process someone chose to show you.

  2. 02Measure the baseline before the ROI

    An efficiency claim without a baseline is self-congratulation.

  3. 03Replace manpower with mechanisms

    Teaming, content and delivery become pipelines that run without someone watching.

  4. 04Use AI as a mirror, not a judge

    On decisions that matter, actively look for the opposing view.

06

Questions

Three things I'm still working out. Judgements age; the evidence can be checked.

  1. aThe value of AI apps sits in orchestration

    Models commoditise; the moat is in workflows, data and habits. So I bet on delivery, not on models.

  2. bThe one-person company is a change in division of labour

    OpenAI's analysis of 800k work messages found 43.5% of job queries doing another profession's work — densest at marketing × engineering.

  3. cAcquisition is changing at the infrastructure level

    Appearance in AI answer pages rose from 15% to 43% in a year. The contest moved from ranking to being cited.
