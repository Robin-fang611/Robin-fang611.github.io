What I'm thinking about
Execution can be replaced · judgment can't

a.The acquisition entry point is being rewritten

AI summaries now appear in 43% of search results, up from 15% in a year. The question is no longer "what rank am I" but "will the AI cite me."

b.The value sits in the orchestration layer

Models will commoditize; the moat is orchestration, data, and workflows. That's why I bet on FDE, not on models.

c.One-person companies aren't romantic — they're measurable

Analyzing 800k work-related messages, OpenAI found 43.5% of job-specific queries involve another occupation's work, densest at the marketing × engineering intersection.

d.AI covers execution, not judgment

A solo operator's edge is client understanding, solution judgment, and trust — not "I can write all the code myself."

e.Plan the acquisition model before you need it

Today it's on-site work, relationships, and a one-page deck — almost zero paid. At scale, marketing mix modeling measures each channel's real ROI instead of assuming the last winning campaign keeps working.
